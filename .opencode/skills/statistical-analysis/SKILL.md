# Statistical analysis
Use actual dates, clicks, impressions, CTR (clicks/impressions) and impression-weighted position. No trend claim without comparable complete windows. Low samples are hypotheses, not causation; missing rows are unknown. Manual AI samples are not platform-wide visibility. Recorded requests are not qualified leads. Prefer the strongest measured issue, not vanity volume. Never invent a benchmark or guaranteed lift.
