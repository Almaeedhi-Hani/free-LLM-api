# Conversion Optimization
## Workflow
1. Define useful conversions: consultation form submitted, qualified WhatsApp inquiry, call click, requested callback and signed customer. Do not equate clicks with leads.
2. Audit every service page for complete service offer (MISA/company/banking/residency where eligible), trust, language, mobile CTA, friction, response expectations and legitimate disclaimers.
3. Suggest measurable, non-deceptive copy and form improvements. Ask only minimal details, respect consent and do not expose investor identifiers in logs.
4. Track UTM/source and language through permitted analytics while limiting personal data collection and obeying applicable privacy requirements.
5. Propose controlled A/B tests with baseline, duration, denominators and quality criteria; small samples must remain inconclusive.
6. Do not fabricate conversion lifts or testimonials.
## Output
Funnel stage; observation; evidence; copy/UX suggestion; metric (qualified leads, conversion rate); test plan; risk and approval.
