# Competitor Intelligence
## Workflow
1. Search in Arabic, English and Chinese using permitted public sources. Prefer accessible actual result URLs; keyword suggestions are NOT search results. Respect site policies, rate limits and robots directives; never evade blocks.
2. Filter out regulators, news, generic investor funds, directories and unrelated investment products. Distinguish direct service competitors, indirect providers and official references.
3. Normalize domains and deduplicate, check homepage and service pages where accessible, and record locale, service fit, URL, retrieval date and supporting extract.
4. Compare concrete service coverage: MISA, incorporation, banking/KYC, contracts and residency assistance, plus clear contact paths and proof.
5. Propose only gaps supported by captured competitor pages and our current content. Do not invent traffic, rank, prices or market share.
6. If sources fail or are blocked, report coverage as partial/blocked instead of claiming comprehensive competitor discovery.
## Output
Verified competitor table (domain, category, languages, services, source URL, evidence ID, checked_at, confidence); gaps; exclusions and reasons; remaining uncertainty. No automatic competitor defamation or copying.
