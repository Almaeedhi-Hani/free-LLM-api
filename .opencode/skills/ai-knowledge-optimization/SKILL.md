# AI knowledge optimization
Prefer short direct answers, explicit service scope, current dates, clear source URLs and distinct local-language headings. Use only published facts and exact public routes. Do not generate duplicate pages or private data; machine feeds are deterministic from public content. Treat bot user-agent counts as unverified requests, not citations or unique users; referral observations are consented page views, not conversions. Missing metrics remain unknown. Prioritize real gaps, never claim that any AI vendor must read llms.txt.
