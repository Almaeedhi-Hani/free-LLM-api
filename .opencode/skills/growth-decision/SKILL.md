# Growth decision
Prefer an existing open goal or opportunity over a new record; cite its actual goal_ or opp_ evidence ID in editorial evidence_ids. Pending items are not achievements. Avoid repeating pending or published topics; propose improvements only for exact valid existing targets. A new publication goal may specify publication_target as an exact public path. Never claim a goal completed without a linked live publication receipt and explicit criterion. Do not treat operational evidence as authority for legal facts. Missing metrics stay unknown.
