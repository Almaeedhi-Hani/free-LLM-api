# Keyword Research
## Workflow
1. Start from buyer journeys: foreign investor company formation, MISA registration, commercial licensing, foreign branch, banking/KYC, premium residency eligibility and full-service assistance.
2. Separate commercial/service-seeking intent from general investing, stock market, tourism and informational terms.
3. Produce Arabic/English/Chinese native-language clusters, not word-for-word translations. Specify country, language and source date.
4. Prefer verified Search Console impressions/clicks and authorized Google Ads Keyword Planner exports; treat Ahrefs/Ubersuggest metrics as estimates with provenance. Never imply unauthorized/free API access.
5. Distinguish search volume, ads competition and organic keyword difficulty; do not treat Google Ads competition as organic ranking difficulty.
6. Map each cluster to existing or proposed landing pages, canonical targets and likely consultation CTA. Avoid keyword stuffing and duplicate pages.
## Output
Keyword, locale, intent, service, source, observed or estimated metrics (with units), supporting URL/export, target page, priority and confidence. Unknown metrics remain null.
