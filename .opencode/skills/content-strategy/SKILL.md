# Content Strategy
## Workflow
1. Ground each brief in a real customer question, keyword intent, service page and verified knowledge gap.
2. Build a useful original structure covering eligibility, documents, process, competent authority, realistic variable costs/timelines, and our assistance boundaries. No copied competitor text.
3. Draft separately in Arabic, English and Simplified Chinese using natural local terminology; flag any needed expert translation review.
4. Include current official sources next to sensitive claims; distinguish facts from suggestions, assumptions and commercial offers.
5. Include one clear consultation/WhatsApp CTA that does not promise guaranteed approvals.
6. Classify licensing, law, fees, visas/residency and banking as high risk. Route to human review; do not publish automatically.
## Output
Target persona; search intent; target page; outline; supported claims with URLs; unanswered questions; draft; risk; review checklist; proposed success metric.
